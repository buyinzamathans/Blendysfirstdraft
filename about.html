Our Story

Blendy's started the way most good bakeries do — with a genuine love of baking and a willingness to say yes to every order, no matter how ambitious the design.

HOW IT STARTED

Baked from scratch, made with intention

Every cake that leaves our kitchen in Muyenga is baked fresh to order — no shortcuts, no pre-made shells sitting in a freezer. From a single cupcake box to a multi-tier wedding cake, each order gets the same care: real ingredients, hand-finished detail, and a design that actually looks like what you asked for.

What began with birthday cakes for friends and family has grown into a full dessert kitchen — wedding cakes, christening cakes, custom character designs, cupcakes, brownies, banana bread and more, all baked in-house.
